hi!

My name is Tori Riemersma, and I am a UX designer and engineer. I currently work in the media space, but have experience in e-commerce and education.

While I have skills spanning the whole UX spectrum, I most enjoy working on the line between 📐 design and 💻 development.

As a designer, I strive to create accessible, logical, useful, and efficient experiences. While aesthetics are undeniably important, I believe prioritizing functionality is the key to the best user experience.

Bio

My interest in technology goes way back, from spending near all of my free time in school on the school computers, learning ways to get around the network blocks to play games and installing a Linux distro on my first-gen iPod nano.

I grew up in the Phoenix metro area and graduated with my 🎓BMus in Music Education from Arizona State University in May of 2016, after which I taught middle school band for a year before leaving education to race and work on bikes.

Shortly after, I moved to Washington, DC. I spent some time working as a bike messenger, but when COVID hit, I had the opportunity for a career shift. I remembered my early passion for technology and taught myself front-end development while also getting my 🎓 Master’s in UX Design from Maryland Institute College of Art, graduating in January 2022.

I contributed to some small open source projects, volunteered design work, and started my career first in a contract role making a difference in the endangered traditional art world at Lost Canyon Imports, before getting hired at 🏢 The Chronicle of Higher Education to work on their digital media, journalism, data and AI products in August 2022.

I realized how much I missed the mountains and relocated to Portland, Oregon in 2024.

In my spare time...

🚵 Compete in self-supported ultracycling races that send me across the world (most recently, a 1250 mile race in Kyrgyzstan).

🚴 Race road and cyclocross at an elite level, now with Nine Lives Cycling.

🐿️ Volunteer with the local wildlife refuge to raise and rehabilitate baby squirrels and other small mammals.

🦎 Hang out with my partner and our menagerie of critters – Bojji, the crested gecko, and Milo, Dale, and Boomy (fancy rats).
